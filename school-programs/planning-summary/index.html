School & Group ProgramsCreate & Celebrate

A creative school day.
A keepsake of their own.

On-site pottery painting: students design a prepared mug, plate or bowl. Elly’s collects, kiln-fires, packs and returns the pieces on an agreed date.

Grades 3–1220–30 students / classStandard 60-minute sessionWeekday visits

Learning & format

Grades 3–5: personal symbols and visual storytelling.
Grades 6–8: pattern, contrast and design planning.
Grades 9–12: sketching, independent design and reflection.

Sample session: demonstrate → plan → paint → share → identify work for collection. We normally arrive 15–30 minutes before the first session. Allow about 15 minutes between classes; pack-up is separate.

What Elly’s provides

One standard ceramic piece per student, painting materials, instruction, collection, kiln firing, packing and one agreed local return delivery. One instructor and 1–2 assistants attend, depending on group size and session needs.

What your school provides

Tables, chairs, water access, room access for setup and a teacher or familiar staff member who stays with the group.
