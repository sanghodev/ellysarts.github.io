01

The experience

What will students make and learn?+−

Students paint a prepared mug, plate or bowl. Guidance for grades 3–12 covers storytelling, pattern and original design. Beginners are welcome.

See studio examples before & after firing →

Can we plan this for a whole grade or through our PTA / PA?+−

Yes. Classes take turns, with one piece per student. Your PTA / PA can request a proposal for school review. Explore project budgets.

02

Your school day

How long is a visit, and who comes?+−

Standard sessions are 60 minutes, with about 15 minutes between classes. One instructor and 1-2 assistants normally arrive 15-30 minutes before the first session. A school teacher or staff member stays with the group. Timing and staffing details.

What does our school need to prepare?+−

Tables, chairs, water access, room access for setup and a familiar staff member. We bring the ceramics and painting materials; table protection and cleanup roles are agreed in advance. Preparation checklist · Cleanup arrangements.

03

Finished pieces & return

When do students receive their finished pieces?+−

Usually about two weeks after painting, on the agreed return date. Tell us your event deadline first. Return delivery details.

What if a piece is damaged, or we have care questions?+−

Contact us with a photo and the piece identifier if something arrives damaged. We confirm appropriate care and use for your selected ceramics. Damage and care guidance.

04

Pricing & school approval

How much does it cost, and what is included?+−

Prices start at $30–$35 per student, with a minimum charge for 20 per class. Standard ceramics, materials, instruction, firing and one agreed local return delivery are included. Whole-grade projects receive a custom quote. View pricing · Print a summary for your meeting.

What happens if students are absent or our date changes?+−

Tell us as soon as possible. Your proposal confirms headcount adjustments and credit or settlement terms; the minimum class charge still applies. Headcounts and credits · Date changes.

05

Groups, locations & celebrations

Which locations and groups do you serve?+−

NYC, select Long Island communities and nearby New Jersey. Schools, community groups, hospital programs and company gatherings are welcome. Share your ZIP, participants’ ages and access or support needs.
